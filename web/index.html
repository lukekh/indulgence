Satura. Haec satura est, nulli ecclesiae coniuncta neque ab ulla probata. Nullus effectus spiritalis promittitur: indulgentiae hic venditae nihil prorsus remittunt.

Pretium verum. Quaeque oratio tesseras (tokens) consumit, quas provisor tuus tibi imputat. Ratio solum textum orationum numerat; usus verus fere bis aut quinquies maior est, in sessionibus longis etiam maior.

Textus generatus. Machina tua orationes componit. Latinitas earum vitiosa, insolita aut theologice dubia esse potest.

Gradus in sessione. Hoc modo machina quae sessionem tuam videt orationes scribit. Effluvia manifesta excernuntur, sed nulla omnino excludi possunt; quam ob rem hae orationes tibi soli ostenduntur.

Opus independens. Neque GitHub neque Anthropic neque Clockwork Labs (SpacetimeDB) coniunctum.

Deletio. Mandatum /indulgence forget rationem tuam, nexus sectatorum, sessiones et candelas delet. Licentiam OAuth quoque in optionibus GitHub tuis quovis tempore revocare potes.

Secretum. Ignoti sacerdotem sine nomine vident orationes paratas recitantem, et nomina in candelis. Amici mutui nomen tuum vident et orationes tuas legunt. Textus orationum evanescit cum sacerdos tuus discedit.

Satire. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any church. No spiritual effect is claimed: indulgences sold here grant no remission of anything.

Real cost. Every prayer spends tokens your provider bills you for. The budget counts prayer text only; actual usage is roughly 2 to 5 times higher, and more in long sessions.

Generated text. Your model writes the prayers. The Latin may be wrong, odd or theologically dubious.

In-session tier. In that mode a model that can see your session writes the prayers. Obvious leaks are filtered but none can be ruled out, which is why those prayers are shown only to you.

Independent project. Not affiliated with GitHub, Anthropic or Clockwork Labs (SpacetimeDB).

Deletion. /indulgence forget deletes your account, follow edges, sessions and candles. You can also revoke the OAuth app in your GitHub settings at any time.

Privacy. Strangers see an anonymous priest saying canned prayers, plus the names on candles. Your mutuals see your name and read your generated prayers. Prayer text disappears when your priest leaves.
